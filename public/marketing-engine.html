We stay helpful until they're ready — on their timing, not ours.

Stage 03 · Convert

How we turn leads into clients

Two clean ways to close — a human call and a focused page that does the selling for us.

01

Discovery Calls

Our main way to close. We get the warm lead on a short call — not a hard sell, just a quick diagnosis of where they're stuck (leads, admin, or losing clients). Then we show how Prospa fixes exactly that. Closers run 5–10 calls a week, aiming for a 25–30% close rate.

Jordan · ArikiSales CallsPipeline
02

Focused Funnel

Every lead magnet and post sends people to one focused landing page built around the single problem they care about — never a busy homepage. We back it with proof (the Sydney client story) and plain ROI: "one extra deal pays for it." The page does the selling, so the call just confirms the fit.

Stage 05 · Reactivate

How we win back past clients

Lowest-hanging fruit, biggest return — reconnect with people who already know us. Ordered easiest to highest impact.

01

Win-Back Emails

The easiest win. They're already on our list, so reaching out costs us nothing. A short series of "we miss you" emails packed with value — a helpful tip, a quick win, or a fresh result from another client. The goal is simple: remind them why they liked us and get a reply. Lowest effort, fastest return.

Ariki · LunaEmailAutomation
02

Personal Check-In

A real, no-pressure message from someone they know at Prospa — a DM or quick call that says "Hey, how's the business going?" No pitch, just a genuine catch-up. Because they already trust us, a personal touch often reopens the door faster than any campaign.

Jordan · TumaiDMCall
03

Comeback Offer

Give them a clear reason to return now. We show what's new and better since they left — new tools, updates, features — paired with a simple welcome-back deal. It removes the risk and makes saying "yes, let's give it another go" an easy call.
